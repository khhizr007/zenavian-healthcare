# SEO launch checklist

1. **Search Console**: add the domain property (DNS TXT verification via Cloudflare), then submit `https://yourdomain/sitemap-index.xml`. Check Coverage after a few days.
2. **Google Business Profile**: create a profile with the real business address and category (e.g. "Veterinary pharmacy" / "Animal feed supplier" as appropriate), verify by postcard/phone, add the website, hours, phone and photos. Use identical name/address/phone everywhere (NAP).
3. **Trade directories**: list the company on relevant Indian poultry and agri directories and associations (e.g. poultry trade portals, IndiaMART, regional poultry associations), always linking to the website.
4. Confirm `site.domain`, canonical URLs and the Organization schema `sameAs` (social links) are filled.
5. Request indexing for Home, Products and the four product pages in Search Console.
6. Re-run Lighthouse/PageSpeed on the live domain and fix anything flagged.
